## Bookmark Atlas 0.20.0: a language model on your network

Optional. Connect a **LlmOnLan farm** (found by itself) or any server that speaks the OpenAI API, in *Settings ▸ Language models*.

- **Place bookmarks by meaning:** the model's vectors replace word counts for the map, topics, links and “similar bookmarks”. Compute them, choose “meaning”, then run Clusters and redraw the Map.
- **Rewrite summaries and tags** (Grooming): read the pages again, let the model write, then preview, apply or undo with two new scripts.
- **Ask** (new tab): what a selection, the filter or a topic shares; a briefing; what is missing; a reading order; or your own question. Numbers in the answer open the bookmarks.
- **Suggest names** for topics (Clusters), and **why?** beside each similar bookmark.
- Nothing a model writes changes a bookmark without a preview. Only titles, summaries, tags and page text are sent, to the server you chose; the password stays in the keychain.

**Tested against a stand-in farm, not a real one.** Please try it on your farm: connection, vectors (the farm needs “Document search” on) and one small batch of summaries first.
