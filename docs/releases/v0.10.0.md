## Bookmark Atlas 0.10.0: the filter everywhere, Threads, and the whole tree

- **The filter bar now works on Tree, Links and Trends too.** Trends are recomputed for what passes the filter.
- **Threads**, at the top of the Links tab: every bookmark on one line, and an arc between two that resemble each other. Arcs above the line cross from one topic to another; arcs below stay inside a topic. Lined up by time, a tall arc is an idea you came back to. Point at a bookmark, click to keep it, read why each link exists, and follow one to the next bookmark.
- **Surprise me** jumps to a link you would not have looked for: across topics, and far apart in time when there is one.
- **The whole tree at once**, in the Tree tab: families, topics and parts from the centre outwards, with one stroke per bookmark on the rim, longer when more recent. Click a branch to open it, a stroke to select the bookmark.

Threads need nothing; the links that cross topics need a clustering (Clusters tab), and the tree drawing needs the tree to be built once.
