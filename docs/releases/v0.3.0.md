## Bookmark Atlas 0.3.0: clusters, and the e-means test

- **New Clusters tab.** Groups your bookmarks by topic from the words of their titles, summaries and tags. "Auto" picks the number of groups; you can also type one.
- Each cluster shows its size, its most telling tags and a few bookmarks. Rename a cluster, or click "show bookmarks" to see it in the list, where you can select it all, tag it or export it.
- **Three methods to choose from:** k-means (default), e-means, and e-means started from k-means++ centres. Runs are kept so you can compare them.
- **The e-means test is done.** On the real 956-bookmark collection e-means and k-means are equal in quality; k-means stays the default. The full report is in `docs/reports/e-means-test.md`.
- The installer now includes NumPy.
