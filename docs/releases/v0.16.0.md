## Bookmark Atlas 0.16.0: the app explains itself

- **Guide** (new tab): what each part of the app is for and how to read it, in short sections. The **?** at the top right opens the section for the tab you are on.
- **Getting started**: five steps on the first screen, ticked off as you go, each with a button that takes you there. Put them away when you no longer need them; the Guide can bring them back.
- **How to get a Notion token**: step by step in Settings, for both kinds of token, with what to do when the token is refused or your table is not listed.
