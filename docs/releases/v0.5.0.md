## Bookmark Atlas 0.5.0: saved views and snapshots

- **New Views tab.** Save where you are (the tab, the search, the filter, the selection, the map position and focus) under a name, with a note. Open it later to come back there.
- **Share a snapshot.** One file with the bookmarks, their tags, the clusters, the map and your saved views. Whole collection, or only the selected bookmarks.
- **Open a snapshot** someone sent you with the menu **Atlas > Open snapshot…**. The app shows it in place of your collection, with a banner saying so; your own collection is not touched. **Atlas > Return to my collection** brings you back.
- A snapshot does not contain your Notion token, your grooming history or the raw records from Notion.

Two ways to share, for two purposes: **Export selection** gives bookmarks to merge into someone's collection; a **snapshot** shows an exploration as you saw it.
