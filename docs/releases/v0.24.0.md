## Bookmark Atlas 0.24.0: Notebooks

A new main screen, **Notebook**: study a set of bookmarks with a language model.

- **Sources, conversation, notes** side by side. Create a notebook from a topic, the selection or the filter, or tick bookmarks and press *New notebook from these*.
- **Answers rest on the sources.** Each statement ends with a number: press it to read the exact words of the source it rests on. Atlas checks that the quoted words really are in the source and sets aside any statement that fails. What the sources do not say is said, not guessed.
- **Finding passages happens on your computer**; only the passages found and the conversation go to the model.
- **Follow-up questions** work, and four ready readings of the whole set (what they share, a briefing, what is missing, a reading order) start a conversation.
- **Notes:** keep an answer or write your own; save them as a Markdown document with quotes and sources.
- The Ask tab is gone: its questions live in the notebook.

Needs a connected language model (Settings ▸ Language models). A model that runs inside the app, with no set-up, is the next release. Tested against a stand-in model only.
