## Bookmark Atlas 0.2.1: a data folder you can move

- **One folder holds everything:** bookmarks, your grooming scripts, backups and the Notion token. Settings shows where it is.
- **Atlas > Choose data folder…** puts it wherever you want (another disk, a synced drive). Your data is copied there and the original is left in place as a safety copy.
- **Changing computer:** copy the folder to the new machine, install Bookmark Atlas, choose the folder. Everything is back.
- If the folder is missing at start (unplugged drive), the app asks what to do and does not create an empty collection behind your back.

The folder holds your Notion token, so do not hand it to someone else. To share bookmarks, use Export selection.
