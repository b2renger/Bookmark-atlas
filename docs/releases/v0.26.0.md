## Bookmark Atlas 0.26.0: the app follows your data, and the notebook talks to the pictures

- **Five stages at the top:** Collect, Clean, Explore, Study, Keep. Each says where you stand ("360 bookmarks", "done once", "1 notebook"), the next useful one is outlined, and the row under it holds that stage's screens with a one-line suggestion. Nothing forces the order.
- **Collect ▸ Sources** is a screen of its own: browsers on this computer, files, Notion. Settings keeps only where your data lives and language models. An empty collection opens on Collect.
- **The set at a glance, in every notebook:** how many sources, over which months, a bar per year, their topics and most used tags. Counted by Atlas, not written by a model.
- **From the notebook to the pictures:** *see them on the map / over time / in the list*; a topic chip opens that topic.
- **Questions about the set itself** ("how many per year?", "which topics?") are answered from those figures, and the statement says it rests on figures Atlas counted.
- **From an answer to the map:** *see its sources on the map* selects the bookmarks an answer rests on.
- **From the pictures to a notebook:** any selection can become a notebook, from every Explore screen.
- **Answered by:** every connection you set up is now kept (this computer, your network, each provider), each with its own key in the system keychain. With two or more, choose who answers each question; every answer names its model. Settings lists them, with *use this one* and *forget*.
- A new screen now starts at its top; the **?** on Settings opens the language-model section of the Guide.
- README pictures retaken, with the stages and a notebook (invented collection, stand-in model).

**What was checked:** 341 automatic tests and browser runs on an invented collection, with stand-in servers playing a farm and a provider. **Not checked:** a real farm, a real provider, or a real model answering from the figures; a small model may misread them, which the quote check catches only when the quoted words are absent.
