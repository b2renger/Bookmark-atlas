## Bookmark Atlas 0.8.0: one filter, an animated map, a timeline and 3D

- **Filter bar** above the list, the map and the grid: tags, site and a range of dates. Set it once, it applies to all three.
- **Four arrangements on the Map**, with the dots gliding from one to the next: by similarity, along a **timeline** with one row per topic, on the grid, and **space and time in 3D**, where the map lies flat and time rises from it. Drag to turn the 3D view.
- **Play** replays your collection as it grew. The slider goes to any day.
- Saved views remember the filter and the arrangement.

The grid arrangement needs a grid: press **Draw the grid** in the Grid tab once. The filter does not yet apply to Tree, Links and Trends.
