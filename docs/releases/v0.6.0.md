## Bookmark Atlas 0.6.0: grid and topic tree

- **New Grid tab** (a self-organising map). Every bookmark sits in one cell and neighbouring cells hold related bookmarks. A larger disc means more bookmarks, so you see where your collection is dense and which themes border which. Click a cell to read it, select its bookmarks or open them in the list. Type words to highlight the cells that match.
- **New Tree tab.** Close topics are gathered into families and large topics are split into parts. Open a pearl to go inside it. Rename anything, and move a topic to another family.
- Saved views remember the Grid and Tree tabs, and snapshots carry the grid and the tree with your changes.

Press **Draw the grid** and **Build the tree** once to make them. The tree is built from the latest clustering.
