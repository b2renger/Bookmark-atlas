## Bookmark Atlas 0.7.1: links and trends

- **New Links tab.** See which topics share the most similar bookmarks. Below the picture: bookmarks that sit between two topics, bookmarks that resemble nothing else, and old bookmarks close to what you gathered lately.
- **Similar bookmarks.** Under every bookmark in the list, a link shows its nearest neighbours.
- **New Trends tab.** Bookmarks saved and published per month, how long after publication you save things, and which topics and tags take more or less room over the last 90 days.
- Clustering is a little steadier: k-means now keeps the best of 10 starts.

Links and the topic part of Trends need a clustering: open **Clusters** and press **Cluster** once. Trends is more complete once bookmarks have their save date, which comes with the Notion sync.

This replaces 0.7.0, which was built by mistake from the commit before these features and does not contain them.
