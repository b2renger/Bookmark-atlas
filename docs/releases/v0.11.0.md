## Bookmark Atlas 0.11.0: grooming loose ends

New in the Grooming tab:

- **Review what changed in Notion**: rows you edited in Notion after importing them. Take Notion's version or keep yours.
- **Merge near-duplicates**: the same thing saved under two links.
- **Merge tag synonyms**: plural and singular, initials and the words they stand for.
- **Check the links**, then **Tag dead links**: pages that are gone get a tag; nothing is deleted.
- Publication dates for **Hugging Face** models, datasets and spaces, and for **YouTube** videos.

The scripts that guess (near-duplicates, synonyms) show every proposal for review, and every run can be undone.
