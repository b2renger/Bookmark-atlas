## Bookmark Atlas 0.12.0: 3D link graph, pictures to keep, and loose ends

- **Links (3D)** on the Map (Arrange by): every bookmark tied to its three closest, floating in space. Watch the shape form, drag to turn, point at a bookmark to light its links.
- **Keep a picture**: under the map, the threads and the tree, "save as image" or "save as web page" (one file you can send to someone).
- **Date brush** in the filter bar: drag across the months to pick dates, double-click to clear.
- **Tree editing**: drag one topic onto another to merge them, split a topic into its parts, or give it its own family.
- The Links tab is now fully recomputed for the filter, and saved views remember the thread you were reading.
