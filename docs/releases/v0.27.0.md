## Bookmark Atlas 0.27.0: the rest of the independent review

0.26.1 fixed the four findings that could lose or expose data. This release deals with the others, and with what the fixes themselves turned up.

**Answers you can lean on**

- **The quote check works on whole words, inside one sentence.** It used to accept "likely to cause harm" against a source saying "unlikely to cause harm", and could join two distant sentences into one quote. It still only proves the words are in the source, not that they are the right proof: read the quote.
- **A failed model request records nothing.** Before, a wrong password could mark hundreds of summaries as "done" with nothing in them. The bookmark now stays to be done, and a job the model refuses entirely stops at once and says why.
- **Pages with unclosed tags are read again.** One unclosed list item in a menu used to hide the whole page.
- **Vectors of two different models are never compared.**

**The model inside the app**

- **The Advanced model can now be downloaded.** The address shipped in 0.25.0 pointed to a file that does not exist. It is now the right one (5.0 GB).
- **Every model file is pinned** by its size and SHA-256 and installed only when whole. A download cut at 60% used to be accepted.
- **The model stops with the app**, even when the app is ended abruptly. It used to keep running and holding memory.

**Privacy and security**

- **Pages are fetched from the public web only.** Odd spellings of local addresses, redirects and names that point inside your network are refused, so a hostile imported file cannot make Atlas read your router or intranet and send that text to a model.
- **Another program on your computer can no longer read the app's page** and, through it, use the app.
- **A snapshot of part of your collection carries nothing about the rest**: no saved view, and no name or note of a topic none of its bookmarks belongs to.
- **Builds are pinned**: the Python, the llama.cpp program and the npm packages the installer is made from.

**Interface**

- "Clusters" is now **Topics**, everywhere. **Workspaces** sits under Keep.
- The stage status follows what you do, without changing screen.
- Stage names stay visible in narrow windows and with the side panel open.
- An empty collection shows the getting-started steps on its first screen.
- From a notebook to the map and **back**: the map says whose sources are selected and offers the way back.
- The per-year chart shows its numbers and says how many sources have no date.
- The former Ask screen is gone; answers it had kept stay readable under Notebooks.
- Results and errors are announced to screen readers.
- Topic names are no longer cut at the edges of the map or around the whole tree.

**Imports**

- Files written by `mysqldump` with its default options are read. A file cut short says so. A CSV that merely mentions SQL stays a CSV. A cell holding a whole article is read.
- A map can be drawn for bookmarks that have no words at all.

**Documentation:** the README is rewritten around the five stages, with one section on privacy and an honest status; the Guide no longer names controls that do not exist.

**Checked:** 434 automatic tests (93 more than at the review), browser runs at four widths, and on the build machines: REPLACE-CI-EVIDENCE

**Not checked, and limits that remain:** a real LlmOnLan farm, a real provider, the Medium and Advanced models actually answering, any model on Windows or macOS. Two links that differ only by their port or by what follows a `#` are still taken for the same link. A Notion import blocks other changes while it runs. The map, the threads and the whole tree cannot be worked with the keyboard alone. The installers are not signed.
