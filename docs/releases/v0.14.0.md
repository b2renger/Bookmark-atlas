## Bookmark Atlas 0.14.0: workspaces, and browser bookmarks

- **Workspaces** (new tab): a copy of part of your collection that lives apart, with its own tags, topics, map, views and Notion table. Start one from a filter, a selection, a topic, everything, or nothing. A banner shows where you are and brings you back.
- **Merge back when you choose.** Merging only adds: the bookmarks your collection lacks, and the tags the workspace gave to those it has. Or delete the workspace; your collection is not affected.
- **Browser bookmarks.** Import the bookmarks file exported by Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge (Settings > Import a file). Folders become tags.
- Any import goes into whatever is open, so a workspace is the place to try a new source before deciding what to keep.
