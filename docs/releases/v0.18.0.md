## Bookmark Atlas 0.18.0: a 3D link graph organised by topic, and a page that fills the window

- **Links · 3D** no longer settles into one even mesh. Each topic gathers into its own cloud, topics that share links sit close to each other, and the others keep their distance, on all three axes.
- **Links that cross topics stand out.** Links inside a topic are a faint texture; the ones that tie two topics together are drawn stronger. Point at a bookmark to light its own links.
- **Depth is readable:** the picture turns slowly (drag it or press *Stop turning* to hold it; it stays still when the system asks for reduced motion), far dots are fainter, and dots shrink when there are thousands.
- **Faster:** about five seconds to settle 3,400 bookmarks, under two for 1,000.
- **The page now fills the window,** centred, and the pictures grow with it. Opening a bookmark's side panel moves the page over instead of covering it, and closing it gives the room back.

Not checked on a real collection of that size: the 3,400-bookmark test used an invented collection.
