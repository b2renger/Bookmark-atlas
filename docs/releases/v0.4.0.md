## Bookmark Atlas 0.4.0: the map

- **New Map tab.** Every bookmark is a dot; bookmarks about similar things sit close together. Topic names are written on the map.
- **Explore:** drag to move, scroll to zoom, hover a dot for its title, click it for the summary, tags and link.
- **Focus a topic** by clicking its name or picking it in "Focus on". **Highlight** bookmarks matching words you type.
- **Select an area** with Shift+drag. The selection is the same one as in the list: tag it, export it, or "show only these in the list".
- Colour shows what you are looking at (focused topic, search matches, selection), not which topic a dot belongs to: sixteen colours would not be told apart, so topics are named instead.
- Drawing the map takes a few seconds and is redone on demand.
