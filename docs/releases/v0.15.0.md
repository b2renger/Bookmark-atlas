## Bookmark Atlas 0.15.0: zoom everywhere, and a side panel for every bookmark

- **Pan and zoom on every picture**: threads, the whole tree, the grid, the picture of topics and the monthly bars now behave like the map. Scroll to zoom, drag to move, double-click to go back.
- **Side panel.** Click a bookmark's name anywhere (the list, a dot on the map, a thread, the tree, a grid cell) to open a panel with its summary, dates, tags and the bookmarks most like it, and a button to **open the page in your browser**. Click a tag to filter on it, a similar bookmark to go on reading. Escape closes the panel.
- Ctrl+click on a name still opens the page directly.
