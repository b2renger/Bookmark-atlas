## Bookmark Atlas 0.25.0: a language model inside the app

- **Settings ▸ Language models ▸ On this computer (built in).** The app now carries its own engine (llama.cpp). Download a model once, press *Use*, and notebooks work with nothing leaving your computer: no account, nothing to install.
- **Three sizes:** Light (0.8 GB, any computer), Medium (3.1 GB, the one to start with on 8 GB of memory), Advanced (5.3 GB, for 16 GB). The page marks the one suggested for your computer.
- **Vectors model** (0.6 GB): finds passages and places bookmarks by meaning. It is the same model a LlmOnLan farm serves, so its vectors remain valid when you move to a farm.
- **Small models kept honest:** the engine can only answer a notebook in the form asked for, and every quote is still checked against the source.
- Downloads resume where they stopped and can be cancelled. A farm or a provider with an API key remain available in the same place.

Without a graphics card, loading takes a minute or two and an answer from half a minute to a few minutes.

**What was really run:** on the Linux build machine, the engine with the Light model and the Vectors model, downloaded from their real addresses, answered one notebook question in 18 seconds: the right passage was found by meaning, one statement was kept and four were set aside because their quotes were not in the source. That is the Light model behaving as described; Medium and Advanced were not run. The engine starts on the Windows and macOS build machines but answered no question there.
